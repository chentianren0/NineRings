# Nine Rings: privacy policy

Nine Rings collects no data about you.

- It does not collect, store or share any personal information.
- It does not track you, and it shows no ads.
- It does not use the network. It has no account, no analytics and no code from other companies.
- The only things it keeps are its own settings, on your device: whether you have seen the welcome page, and whether sounds, haptics and Follow the Move are on. They stay on the device, and are removed when you delete the app.

The app's Support and Privacy Policy links open these pages in your browser. The app itself sends nothing.

## Questions

Ask on the project's issues page: https://github.com/chentianren0/new_nine_rings_nov/issues. What you write there is public, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies to it.
