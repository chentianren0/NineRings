# Nine Rings: privacy policy

Nine Rings collects no data about you.

- It does not collect, store or share any personal information.
- It does not track you, and it shows no ads.
- It has no account, no analytics and no code from other companies.
- It keeps its settings on your device.
- It keeps the toy as you left it and the plays you finish in your iCloud Drive when iCloud Drive is on, so that they follow you to your other devices, and on your device otherwise. The developer cannot see them.
- When you are signed in to Game Center, it reports the achievements you earn there.

The app's Support and Privacy Policy links open these pages in your browser.

## Questions

Ask on the project's issues page: https://github.com/chentianren0/NineRings/issues. What you write there is public, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies to it.
