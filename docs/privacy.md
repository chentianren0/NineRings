# Nine Rings: privacy policy

Nine Rings collects nothing about you. What it keeps is on your devices and, while iCloud Sync is on, in your own iCloud. None of it reaches us.

- We run no server. The only services the app uses are Apple's: iCloud and Game Center.
- It has no account of its own, no analytics and no ads, and it does not track you.
- It includes no code from other companies.

## What the app keeps

- The toy as you left it, with the play in progress and the achievements you are on the way to, so that you can go on where you stopped.
- Your finished plays, as History shows them: when each was played, its moves, its undos and the moves played for you, how long it took, whether it was on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and the version of the app.
- Which achievements you have earned, and which of them Game Center has taken.
- Its settings: whether you have seen the welcome page, and the switches in Settings.
- A random number for this copy of the app, which lets your devices tell their toys apart. It says nothing about you or your device.

All of it is kept on your device. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes the toy, the plays, the achievements and the settings from the device.

## iCloud

iCloud Sync is on at first, and its switch is in Settings. While it is on and iCloud is available, your finished plays and each device's toy, with its random number, are also kept in your own iCloud account, and reach your other devices through it. They are in your account, not ours, and we cannot see them. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers iCloud.

While the switch is off, or iCloud is not available, the app sends nothing to iCloud. Turning the switch off copies your plays to the device and leaves what is in iCloud as it is.

Clearing the history while iCloud Sync is on deletes your plays on all your devices and in iCloud. While it is off, it deletes them on this device only, and the plays still in iCloud come back when you turn it on.

Deleting the app does not delete what it keeps in iCloud. To delete that too, turn iCloud Sync off on your devices, then delete the app's data in your iCloud settings.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app reports the achievements you earn to Game Center, Apple's service, which shows them in your Game Center profile. It sends nothing else, it reads no identifier of yours, and nothing reaches us. An achievement earned while you are not signed in waits on your device and is reported when you sign in. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers Game Center, and you can sign out of it in Settings.
